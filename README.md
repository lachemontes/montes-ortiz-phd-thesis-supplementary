# Supplementary material: Montes-Ortiz PhD thesis (Lund University, 2026)

Thesis: Evolution of multigene families in insect and non-insect arthropods:
From chemosensory receptors to genome-wide specialization
Defense: 4 December 2026, Department of Biology, Lund University

## Contents
- Paper_I/    Argiope bruennichi: curated receptor sequences, phylogenetic trees,
              chromosome maps, expression tables (TPM, DESeq2), supplementary figures
- Paper_II/   Ixodes ricinus: curated receptor sequences, trees, expression data,
              supplementary figures
- Kappa/      Analyses performed for the thesis summary: GR C-terminal motif
              analysis (MEME, CD-HIT, shuffled control, TOMTOM, FIMO outputs)
              and source data and scripts for the summary figures

Paper III is published in BMC Genomics (2026) 27:549,
https://doi.org/10.1186/s12864-026-13049-3, with its own supplementary material.

## Status
Papers I and II are unpublished manuscripts. Data are provided for evaluation of
the thesis. Please contact the author before reuse.

## Citation
Montes-Ortiz, Z., Powell, D., Vogel, H., Löfstedt, C., & Andersson, M. N. (2026). Comparative genomics reveal signatures of ecological specialization in the striped ambrosia beetle Trypodendron lineatum. BMC genomics, 27(1), 549.
